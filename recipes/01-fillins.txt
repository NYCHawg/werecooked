- 1 cup butterscotch chips (optional topping)

# Classic Yellow Cheese Dip | 8 | 20 | appetizer | meal | Arkansas Classics, Family Recipes | cheese dip, arkansas
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1 lb white American or Velveeta, cubed
- 1 cup sharp cheddar, shredded
- 1 can (10 oz) Rotel diced tomatoes and green chiles, drained
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp pickled jalapeño juice

> Slowly whisk in the milk and cook until slightly thickened, 3–4 minutes.

! Arkansas is the cheese dip state — it was first served at Mexico Chiquito in Hot Springs in 1935.
