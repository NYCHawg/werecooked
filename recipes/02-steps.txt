# Red Wine Braised Pork Chops with Garlic | 6 | 50 | main | meal | Family Recipes |
> Heat the oil and butter in a heavy skillet over high heat. Salt and pepper the chops and sear about 2 minutes per side until golden. Set aside.
> Lower the heat to medium-high, add the garlic cloves and cook 2 minutes, stirring, until they start to color.
> Pour in the wine and broth, add the bay leaf and balsamic, and scrape up the browned bits. Boil 3 minutes.
> Return the chops, cover, and simmer on low 20–25 minutes until tender, adding broth if it gets dry.
> Move the chops to a platter, boil the sauce until syrupy, swirl in the extra tablespoon of butter and pour over the chops with the soft garlic.

# Baked Mac and Cheese | 8 | 55 | side | meal | Family Recipes, Thanksgiving |
> Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9x13 dish. Toss the cooked macaroni with the olive oil.
> Melt the 6 tbsp butter, whisk in the flour and cook 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and cream and simmer until thick, about 5 minutes.
> Off the heat, stir in 3 cups cheddar and 1 1/2 cups Gruyère until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
> Fold in the macaroni and pour half into the dish. Sprinkle with the remaining cheddar and Gruyère, add the rest of the pasta.
> Toss the panko with melted butter, Parmesan and paprika and scatter over the top.
> Bake 25–30 minutes until bubbly and golden. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
