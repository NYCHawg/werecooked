# Cathead Biscuits | 8 | 30 | bread | meal | Arkansas Classics, Family Recipes | biscuits, breakfast
- 3 cup self-rising flour (White Lily if you can find it)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup cold butter, cubed
- 1/4 cup cold shortening
- 1 1/4 cup cold buttermilk
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
> Heat the oven to 450°F and butter a cast iron skillet.
> Whisk the flour and salt, then cut in the butter and shortening until the pieces are pea-sized.
> Stir in the buttermilk with a fork just until a shaggy dough forms.
> Pinch off big handfuls (cat-head size), round them gently and set them touching in the skillet.
> Bake 18–22 minutes until tall and golden. Brush with melted butter.

# Arkansas Smoked Pulled Pork | 12 | 600 | main | meal | Arkansas Classics | barbecue, smoker
- 1 pork shoulder / Boston butt (8 lb)
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard
- 1 recipe BBQ Dry Rub
- 1 cup apple juice (spritz)
- 1/2 cup cider vinegar (spritz)
- 1 recipe Arkansas Barbecue Sauce
- 12 buns and Arkansas BBQ Coleslaw
> Rub the pork with mustard, coat heavily with rub and refrigerate overnight.
> Smoke at 250°F over hickory, fat side up, spritzing with apple juice and vinegar every hour after the first 3 hours.
> When it reaches 165°F and the bark is dark (about 6 hours), wrap tightly in butcher paper or foil.
> Continue until it reaches 203°F and a probe slides in like butter, 3–4 more hours.
> Rest wrapped in a cooler for at least 1 hour, then pull, discarding the bone and fat.
> Serve on buns topped with slaw and sauce, the Arkansas way.
