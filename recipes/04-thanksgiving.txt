# Classic Roast Turkey | 12 | 240 | main | meal | Thanksgiving, Holiday & Desserts | turkey, holiday
- 1 whole turkey (12–14 lb), thawed, giblets removed and saved
- 3 tbsp kosher salt (dry brine)
- 1 tbsp brown sugar (dry brine)
- 2 tsp black pepper (dry brine)
- 1 tsp dried thyme (dry brine)
- 1 tsp dried sage (dry brine)
- 1 stick butter, softened
- 1 onion, quartered
- 1 lemon, halved
- 1 head garlic, halved
- 1 bunch fresh thyme, sage and rosemary
- 2 cup chicken or Turkey Stock
> Two or three days ahead: pat the turkey dry, rub the dry brine all over and under the breast skin, and refrigerate uncovered on a rack.
> Take it out 1 hour before roasting. Heat the oven to 425°F.
> Rub the butter under and over the skin. Stuff the cavity with onion, lemon, garlic and herbs. Tuck the wings and tie the legs.
> Roast on a rack over the stock for 30 minutes, then lower to 325°F.
> Roast about 13 minutes per pound total, basting every 45 minutes and tenting the breast with foil once golden, until the thigh reads 170°F and the breast 160°F.
> Rest at least 30 minutes before carving. Save the drippings for Giblet Gravy.

# Cajun Fried Turkey | 12 | 90 | main | meal | Thanksgiving, Arkansas Classics | turkey, fried, outdoor
- 1 whole turkey (12–14 lb), thawed and very dry, giblets removed
- 1/2 cup butter, melted (injection)
- 1/2 cup chicken broth (injection)
- 2 tbsp hot sauce (injection)
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (injection)
- 2 tbsp Arkansas Cajun Seasoning Blend, divided
- 3 gal peanut oil
> Measure the oil first: put the turkey in the empty pot, cover with water by 1 inch, remove the turkey and mark the water line. Dry the pot and turkey completely.
> Strain the injection (butter, broth, hot sauce, Worcestershire, 1 tbsp seasoning) and inject all over the breast, thighs and legs. Rub the rest of the seasoning on the skin. Refrigerate overnight.
> Outdoors, away from the house, on level ground: heat the oil to 350°F.
> Turn the burner off, lower the turkey in very slowly on the hook, then relight and keep the oil at 325–350°F.
> Fry 3 1/2 minutes per pound until the breast reads 160°F and thigh 175°F.
> Lift out slowly, drain and rest 20 minutes before carving.
! Never fry a frozen or wet turkey, never fry indoors or on a deck, and keep a fire extinguisher close.

- 2 carrots, chunked

# Giblet Gravy | 12 | 30 | sauce | component | Thanksgiving | gravy
- turkey pan drippings
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cup Turkey Stock
- cooked giblets and neck meat, finely chopped
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp poultry seasoning
> Pour the drippings into a cup and spoon off the fat; use it in place of some of the butter.
> Melt the butter and fat in a saucepan, whisk in the flour and cook 3–4 minutes until golden.
> Slowly whisk in the stock and the defatted drippings and simmer 10 minutes until thick.
> Stir in the giblets, eggs, salt, pepper and poultry seasoning. Thin with stock as needed.

# Cornbread Dressing | 12 | 90 | side | meal | Thanksgiving, Arkansas Classics, Family Recipes | dressing, holiday
- 1 recipe Skillet Cornbread, baked a day ahead and crumbled
- 4 slice day-old white bread, torn (or 6 biscuits)
- 1 stick butter
- 2 onion, diced
- 4 celery stalks, diced
- 1 tbsp rubbed sage
- 2 tsp poultry seasoning
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 cup chicken or Turkey Stock, warm
- 3 large eggs, beaten
- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of chicken soup (optional)
> Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9x13 dish.
> Melt the butter and cook the onion and celery 8–10 minutes until soft.
> In a big bowl, combine the cornbread, bread, vegetables and seasonings.

> Spread in the dish and bake 45–55 minutes until set and golden on top.

# Sweet Potato Casserole | 12 | 75 | side | meal | Thanksgiving, Family Recipes | sweet potatoes, holiday

# Yeast Dinner Rolls | 24 | 180 | bread | meal | Thanksgiving, Family Recipes | rolls, baking
- 1 cup whole milk, warm (110°F)
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp) instant yeast
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup butter, melted, plus more for brushing
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 5 cup all-purpose flour
> Stir the milk, water, yeast and sugar and let it foam 5 minutes.
> Mix in the butter, eggs and salt, then the flour a cup at a time to a soft dough. Knead 6–8 minutes.
> Cover and let rise 1 1/2 hours until doubled.
> Shape into 24 balls and set in two buttered 9x13 pans. Rise 45 minutes.
> Bake at 375°F for 15–18 minutes until golden. Brush with butter.

# Divinity | 36 | 45 | dessert | meal | Holiday & Desserts | candy, holiday
- 2 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup light corn syrup
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 large egg whites, room temperature
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup pecans, chopped
> Make this on a dry, low-humidity day. Line pans with wax paper.
> Cook the sugar, corn syrup and water to 260°F without stirring.
> Meanwhile beat the egg whites to stiff peaks. With the mixer running, pour in the hot syrup in a thin stream.
> Beat 5–6 minutes until it holds its shape and loses its gloss. Fold in the vanilla and pecans.
> Drop by spoonfuls onto wax paper and let set.

# Turkey Tetrazzini | 8 | 60 | main | meal | Thanksgiving, Quick Meals | leftovers, turkey

- 4 tbsp butter
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cup Turkey Stock or chicken broth
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/2 cup Parmesan, grated
- 4 cup cooked turkey, chopped
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup mozzarella, shredded
