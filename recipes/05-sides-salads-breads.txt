# Southern Macaroni Salad | 8 | 25 | salad | meal | Side Dishes | cookout, make ahead
- 1 lb elbow macaroni
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard
- 2 tbsp sweet pickle relish
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
- 1/4 cup small red onion, diced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- paprika to garnish
> Cook the macaroni in well-salted water until just tender. Drain and rinse under cold water until cool.
> Whisk the mayonnaise, mustard, relish, vinegar, sugar, salt and pepper in a large bowl.
> Fold in the macaroni, eggs, celery, bell pepper and onion.
> Chill at least 2 hours. Stir, loosen with a spoonful of mayo if needed, and dust with paprika.

# Seven-Layer Salad | 10 | 25 | salad | meal | Side Dishes, Holiday & Desserts | potluck, make ahead
- 1 head iceberg lettuce, chopped
- 1 cup celery stalks, diced
- 1/2 cup small red onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 2 cup frozen peas, thawed
- 1 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 cup shredded cheddar
- 8 slice bacon, cooked and crumbled
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
> Layer the lettuce, celery, onion, bell pepper and peas in a clear trifle bowl or 9x13 dish.
> Stir the mayonnaise and sugar together and spread it over the peas, sealing to the edges.
> Top with the cheddar, bacon and eggs. Cover and chill overnight. Toss at the table or serve in layers.

# Angel Biscuits | 24 | 40 | bread | meal | Family Recipes, Thanksgiving | rolls, make ahead
- 1 (1/4 oz) packet active dry yeast
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 5 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup cold shortening
- 2 cup buttermilk
- 3 tbsp butter, melted
> Dissolve the yeast in the warm water for 5 minutes.
> Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt. Cut in the shortening until crumbly.
> Stir in the buttermilk and yeast just until a dough forms. (Cover and chill up to 5 days if you like.)
> Roll 1/2 inch thick on a floured board, cut with a biscuit cutter and set on a buttered sheet. Let rise 30 minutes.
> Bake at 425°F for 10–12 minutes. Brush with melted butter.

# Southern Spoon Bread | 6 | 55 | bread | meal | Thanksgiving, Arkansas Classics | heirloom
- 3 cup whole milk
- 1 cup white or yellow cornmeal
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 large eggs, separated
- 1 tsp baking powder
> Heat the oven to 375°F and butter a 2-quart dish.
> Scald the milk, whisk in the cornmeal and salt, and cook, stirring, until thick like grits. Stir in the butter and cool 10 minutes.
> Stir in the yolks and baking powder. Beat the whites to soft peaks and fold in.
> Bake 35–40 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve with a spoon, right away.

- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
> Melt the butter in a skillet, add the corn and its milky scrapings, and cook 5 minutes.
> Whisk the cream, milk, sugar and flour; stir into the corn with the salt and pepper.
> Simmer, stirring, 10–15 minutes until thick and creamy.
