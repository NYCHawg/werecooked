## simple-syrup | Simple syrup (1:1) | syrup | 30 | 650
- 400 g white sugar @sugar-white
- 400 g water
> Stir the sugar into hot (not boiling) water until fully dissolved. Cool, bottle and refrigerate.
> By weight, not volume. 1:1 is the default for shaken sours if the spec doesn't say rich.

## rich-simple | Rich simple syrup (2:1) | syrup | 90 | 625
- 600 g white sugar @sugar-white
- 300 g water
> Warm gently and stir until clear. Cool and bottle.
> Use ¾ as much as 1:1 for the same sweetness, with less dilution. Lasts longer: the sugar is a preservative.

## gomme-syrup | Gomme (gum) syrup | syrup | 60 | 800

- 600 g white sugar @sugar-white
- 300 g water (for the syrup)

## orgeat | Orgeat | syrup | 21 | 700
- 250 g blanched almonds @orgeat-almonds
- 500 g water
- 450 g white sugar @sugar-white
- 15 g orange flower water
- 30 g brandy or vodka
- 1 drop bitter almond extract (optional)
> Toast the almonds lightly at 350°F for 5 minutes. Blend with the water until fine and let sit 1 hour.
> Strain through a nut-milk bag, squeezing hard. Weigh the almond milk and add sugar 1:1 by weight; stir to dissolve without heat.
> Add the orange flower water and spirit. Shake before use.
> Mai Tai, Japanese Cocktail, Army & Navy.
