We're Cooked

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

We're Cooked is a private family app for planning meals, tracking the pantry and building shopping lists. It is run by Keith Carter for his household. This policy explains what the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: your name and email address from Google sign-in, used to sign you in and show you to your household.
  • Things you add: recipes, meal plans, pantry items, shopping lists, spending notes and photos you upload.
  • Google Calendar (optional, read-only): if you connect your calendar, the app reads event titles and times from the calendars you choose, for the next four weeks. It uses them only to work out which nights you're away for dinner, and saves just the date and the shift name (for example "Keith Close"). It never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.
  • Location (optional): used only on your device to find nearby restaurants. It is not stored.

How it's used

Your information is used only to run the app's features for you and your household. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising. Members of your household can see the household's shared meal plan, pantry, recipes and lists, including which nights you're away.

Google user data

We're Cooked's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used to train AI models and is not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide the app.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and file hosting). Photos you choose to scan may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to read recipes, receipts or groceries; they are not used for advertising.

Your choices

You can disconnect Google Calendar any time from Family › Work schedule, which deletes the saved calendar access and working nights. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your account and data, email the address below.
